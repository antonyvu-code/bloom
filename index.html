WHY A GARDEN

A design portfolio usually shows finished things. This one shows the habit underneath them — the daily research ritual that feeds every project on this site. No two flowers are alike, because no two mornings are: each bloom's shape and colour are generated from that day's signal, not chosen by hand.

HOW IT GROWS

Canvas 2D, no external renderer. Every stem and petal is tweened with GSAP against a plain data object — growth is a number between 0 and 1, eased with elastic.out, redrawn every frame. Colour comes from a small hash so the palette stays warm and consistent while every flower still looks singular.

The research this garden is grown from is public, daily, and real.
